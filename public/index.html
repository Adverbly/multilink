      Allow pop-ups for once, and every multilink opens all of its links for you.

      Chrome
      1. Click the pop-up blocked icon at the right end of the address bar.
      2. Choose Always allow pop-ups and redirects from .
      3. Click Done, then reload this page.

      On Android: tap Always show in the message at the bottom of the screen.

      Safari
      1. In the menu bar, choose Safari → Settings, then click Websites.
      2. Click Pop-up Windows in the sidebar.
      3. Set to Allow, then reload this page.

      On iPhone or iPad: open the Settings app, go to Safari (or Apps → Safari), and turn off Block Pop-ups.

      Firefox
      1. Look for the yellow bar just below the address bar that says Firefox prevented this site from opening pop-ups.
      2. Click Settings (or Options) on that bar.
      3. Choose Allow pop-ups for , then reload this page.
      Edge
      1. Click the pop-up blocked icon at the right end of the address bar.
      2. Choose Always allow pop-ups and redirects from .
      3. Click Done, then reload this page.
